# A noticing

What goes in the report's §2, and what a lens reader hands back as a candidate. One
definition, read by both lens readers and by the triager, so the three cannot drift.

**This section is to be looked at, not believed.** It carries the things that do not fit, at a
deliberately low standard of proof, so that somebody who knows what this is for can recognise
a shape nobody here can name. Blind readers working to these rules have produced real entries
and none padded, so the rules below are load-bearing rather than style. In a pre-registered
control they also filled §2 at the same rate on a window that had converged as on one still
looping: §2 finds what does not add up, and it does not tell you that caused the loop. A person is good at connecting dots they can see and blind to
dots they cannot, and nobody reads twenty thousand lines of a five-round repair by hand.

The rules all matter:

- **At most five, and that is a ceiling rather than a quota.** Reporting one is a fine run and
  reporting none is a real result. An unbounded list of vague unease is worse than silence, and
  it reproduces the failure this report is about in a softer voice.
- **Each must be recognisable without opening the repository.** Write the discrepancy in the
  language of the thing rather than the code: *"two of these repairs disagree about whether a
  library loan belongs to the reader or to the school that issued the card."* **That example is
  deliberately from another domain.** An illustration drawn from the repository under review
  hands the reader an answer shaped like the one they are meant to derive, and a reader shown
  one has had to discount its own strongest entry. What disqualifies an entry is being stated in
  code terms, **not** being provable. The best noticings are both: the shape is what the owner
  recognises, and hard evidence usually sits right under it. **Being confirmable does not move
  it to the diagnosis.** A section that exiles everything it can prove keeps only the weakest
  half of what the lenses found, which is the opposite of the point.
- **Mark the shape `NOTICED` and its evidence by what it is.** The shape asserts nothing and
  asks somebody to look, so it is never `CONFIRMED`; the two lines under it that show the
  contradiction may well be. Carry both. Follow each entry with one clause saying what would
  make it go away, so a reader who knows better can dismiss it in a sentence.
- **State it as two of something, never as a category.** *"One document says this key is whose
  watched mark it is; another says it is which server the download came from"* is an entry.
  *"This could be a missing distinction"* is the diagnosis label wearing a soft voice, and it
  is the failure mode to watch for here: a category restated as unease tells the owner nothing
  they can recognise. If you cannot name the two things and where each is written down, you do
  not have an entry yet.
- **Say which lens turned each one up, and name the lenses that turned up nothing.** A lens
  that found nothing is a result and it stops the next person re-running it.
- **Say how many of the two readers found it, and mark a disagreement as one.** Two blind
  readers ran the lenses over this window. An entry both reached is strong. An entry one
  reached while the other read the same artifact and cleared it is
  **stronger still as a disagreement**, and it is written as one: the discrepancy, both
  readings, and who can settle it. Do not arbitrate it and do not average it. An entry only
  one reader got to, where the other never looked, says so and stands on its own merits.

**None of these is a work item and none of them is to be patched.** Reaching for the site
where a discrepancy shows is the reflex that built the loop. They exist to generate a
question, and the question belongs to a person.

If every lens came back empty, say that in a line. **It is not a clean bill, and it does not
say where the loop is:** the lenses may have read past it, or it may sit outside the record
entirely — a conversation, a review thread, a decision taken elsewhere, or live behaviour
nobody ran.
