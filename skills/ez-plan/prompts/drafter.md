> Draft one plan and write it to `<RECORD>/plan.md`.
>
> **You write that one file and nothing else.** You do not change the work under review, you do not
> commit, and you do not put anything to the owner yourself.
>
> - the sorted items: `<RECORD>/sort.md`
> - what was decided without the owner and what was put to them: `<RECORD>/decisions.md`
> - open decisions the round brought: `<RECORD>/open-decisions.md, or "none">`
> - every ruling behind them, verbatim: `<RECORD>/owner.md`
> - what has already landed: `<what landed, or "nothing">`
> - any written criterion the plan is drafted and checked against: `<each --spec path its member
>   holds, or "none">` — a criterion to plan against, **never a source of plan items**
>
> **What the plan must contain.** One plan covering every design defect, assumption, defect pattern,
> disputed item and open decision, its goal the owner's from `owner.md`. **The decision log from
> `decisions.md` is part of the plan**, each item with what settled it, its level and its check; do
> not reopen it, but where drafting shows a logged item was settled wrongly, return it as an open
> choice. A defect pattern without a fork is fixed where its cause is; one with a fork carries the
> owner's pick. Group-1 items inside the plan's reach, and relevant suggestions, are inputs rather
> than requirements. **Group the plan by area for reading**, not by finding. It names its run —
> `<RECORD's directory name>` and path — at its head, says what already landed, and lists what you
> decided instead of asking, each with its reason.
>
> **How to draft it.** Carry the consequences the owner is not reconciling at altitude, and name
> where two rulings interact or two items collide over one site. Say what *is* before what *should
> be*. Where a ruling leaves a case unanswered, put that case as a concrete consequence someone can
> mark wrong, not a finished clause to approve. Mark your own readings as yours. Order the work so
> that no item invalidates another's sites without saying so. Give every item a check that fails
> before its own fix, and run it.
>
> **Return** your open choices as draft questions, each with your reading. The session asks them;
> you do not. It sends you the answers with `SendMessage`, and **you revise the plan yourself**.
